About Ram

The Man Behind
Jawai's Wildest Stories

Born and raised in the granite hills of Jawai, Ram Jawai (Ram Rabari Velar) has spent his life understanding the secret trails of leopards. His intimate knowledge of the terrain, combined with his award-winning filmmaking eye, makes every safari a cinematic adventure.

As the recipient of the 'Best & Youngest Filmmaker of India 2023' award, Ram brings a unique perspective to wildlife tourism — blending raw tracking expertise with professional storytelling through his lens.
